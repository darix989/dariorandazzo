IT IS OVER 9000! That's how [Well Bookmarked](/dariorandazzo/projects/well-bookmarked/) started.

Okay, they weren't actually over 9000. They were about 3,000. Still a lot.

I'm the type of person who wants everything I might need within reach. I'm also the type who forgets… anything. So I bookmark whatever.

The problem with that: where the hell did I put it? What was it called? Which folder was it in?

A good example is Amazon's Contact Us page. I needed it often enough to save it, and never often enough to remember where it lived. Search for "amazon" and you get a pile of product pages, seller dashboards, and old orders. The one link I actually wanted was buried in there somewhere.

I looked at other extensions. Nicer UI, same idea: a prettier window over the same mess. No killer feature. Nothing that would make "amazon contact us" show up when I needed it, without me remembering the title, the folder, or the exact URL.

So I built my own.

The idea was simple. I can't remember everything, but maybe I can remember a few keywords — a few tags.

Spoiler: if you get too many tags, you forget those too.

I tagged that Amazon page with `help` and `eshop`. Now I type one of those, and it comes up when I need it. That's the whole pitch, really.

Usually I start a project, get super committed, and quit after two weeks. This time I didn't quit, because I was actually using the thing I had built. My daily routine still relies on it. Trial, error, use, improve — it was a loop then, and I still can't break it.

Once the setup felt pretty good, another problem showed up: duplicate bookmarks. Same URL, saved three times, sitting in three different folders, because I had forgotten it was already there. So I added a way to spot them and get rid of them. There is no cure for my bookmark Alzheimer's, but this helped.

I still use it every day. If your bookmark bar looks like mine used to, give it a few days. Hopefully it clicks for you too.

The tool is free, and it will likely stay that way. If it helps, consider leaving a rating on the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/well-bookmarked/ickglfbpinekdkphoppfigdnccgnnajh) or [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/well-bookmarked/).

Happy bookmarking!
